Terms of Service

What we agree to, what you agree to, and what happens when something goes wrong. Twelve short sections instead of twelve long ones.

On this page

    1. What these terms cover

    These terms apply to every order, quotation and enquiry handled by Amritha Traditional Spice Mill Pvt Ltd, whether it is placed online, by phone, by email or through the trade desk. Placing an order means accepting them, and they apply alongside any written agreement we hold with you — where the two differ, the written agreement wins for that order only.

    Nothing here affects your statutory rights as a consumer under Indian law, and nothing here is intended to limit anything you cannot lawfully limit.

    2. Quotations and prices

    • A quotation is valid for 30 days from the date it is issued, except where it says otherwise on its face.
    • Spice prices move with the harvest. If a lot changes materially between quotation and dispatch we will contact you before milling, and you may cancel without charge.
    • Indicative prices published on this site are starting points. A firm figure requires a lot, a granule profile, a quantity and a packaging choice.
    • We do not issue retrospective price rises. What we quoted on the day is what you pay, unless you ask us to change something.

    3. Orders and acceptance

    Your order is an offer. It becomes a contract when we confirm it in writing, which normally happens within one working day. We may decline an order — for example if a lot fails an incoming test — and if we do, any payment taken is refunded in full within five working days.

    We reserve the right to split a shipment where stock for part of an order is available sooner, and to tell you which part is on its way. Nothing is split without a conversation.

    4. Minimums and lead times

    • Minimum quantities are stated on each service brief and are part of the order, not a guideline.
    • Lead times begin when the raw material is accepted, not when the order is placed. Sourcing can add time in a poor season.
    • Orders inside the published turnaround may attract a rush charge, which we will always quote before you commit.
    • We do not hold stock for an unconfirmed order. If you need a date held, ask for it in writing and we will hold it for seven days.

    5. What we sell, and what that means

    Our spice is single origin, cold stone ground, and contains nothing added. Because it is not blended to a uniform commercial specification, small natural variation between lots is normal — colour, aroma intensity and granule distribution can shift from harvest to harvest. That is the product, not a defect in it.

    Every pack carries a batch card naming the district, grower, harvest window, grind date and measured batch temperature. Keep it: it is how we trace a complaint, and how you can reproduce a lot you liked.

    6. Claims, and what we do about them

    • Tell us within 14 days of delivery if a batch is not right, and we will re-mill it at no charge.
    • For a genuine milling error we also cover freight in both directions.
    • Claims about aroma or flavour after the pack has been opened, decanted or stored in a warm or humid environment cannot be assessed fairly, and we will say so rather than guess.
    • Keep the batch card and the packaging. Both make a claim straightforward and both are thrown away in ten seconds if you do not.

    7. Payment and trade terms

    Retail orders are paid before dispatch. Trade customers on approved credit terms are invoiced on dispatch and payable within 30 days of the invoice date. Interest at 1.5% per month applies to overdue trade accounts, and we will always try to talk to you before we charge it.

    Credit approval is at our discretion, may be reviewed or withdrawn, and is not a commitment to continue supplying. We do not factor invoices, and we do not supply against a disputed invoice.

    8. Delivery, risk and insurance

    • Risk passes to you on delivery to the address you gave us, or on collection.
    • Consignments are insured to the invoice value in transit. We claim against the courier ourselves, and you do not need to chase them.
    • Please inspect a consignment on arrival and photograph any visible damage to the outer packaging before signing. It takes ten seconds and it is the difference between a smooth replacement and an argument.
    • Force majeure — including weather, port disruption and epidemic restrictions — will move lead times. We will tell you, with a new date, rather than let you find out on the due date.

    9. Blend ownership and confidentiality

    You own the recipe you pay us to develop, and we will not sell it, publish it, or use it for another customer. Equally, our process knowledge, our machinery settings, our sourcing contacts and the techniques described in this site's Journal remain ours.

    If a blend is developed jointly — where a customer contributes a component or a technique — we will agree in writing who owns what before development starts, not after a dispute starts.

    10. Liability

    We are responsible for loss or damage caused by our own negligence, for food that does not meet its stated specification, and for anything we have adulterated or contaminated. Our total liability on any order is limited to the value of that order.

    We are not liable for indirect or consequential loss — including lost profit, lost covers and business interruption — except where the law makes that limitation unenforceable, or where our negligence caused a food-safety incident, which is not capped at the order value.

    11. Law and disputes

    These terms are governed by the laws of India, and the courts at Ernakulam, Kerala have jurisdiction. We would much rather sort it out than litigate it, and our standing offer is that we will meet you at the mill, taste the batch together, and settle it on the spot where that is possible.

    If a dispute cannot be settled that way, we will ask you to put it in writing. A written complaint is answered in five working days by someone with the authority to resolve it.

    12. Changes to these terms

    We may update these terms as the business changes. The date at the top of the page is the last revision, and the version that applies to your order is the one in force when the order was confirmed. Changes do not apply retroactively to orders already accepted.

    BEFORE YOU SIGN ANYTHING

    Ask for a Trade Agreement Instead

    Registered kitchens, hotels and distributors usually work better under a short written agreement covering volumes, credit and blend confidentiality, rather than these general terms alone. We draft it, and we will send ours to your lawyer without being awkward about it.

    If anything here does not match how your business works, say so. Terms nobody has read are the ones that cause trouble.
