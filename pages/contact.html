What a visit looks like

  1. 1. Coffee at the packing bench, and a look at the current harvest lots.
  2. 2. Through to the mill floor. You will hear the stones before you see them.
  3. 3. A comparative tasting: same spice, four granule profiles, no commentary from us.
  4. 4. Sit with the trade desk and talk volumes, packaging and dates.

Before you come

  • Closed shoes on the mill floor, please.
  • No photography in the lab room.
  • Shoes and coats are stored at the gate.
  • Groups over six need a week’s notice.

Not in Kochi?

We ship to 34 countries and run a sample programme. Ask for the sample box and we will send four 100 g packs so you can test before committing to a pallet.
