Privacy Policy

What we collect, why we collect it, who else sees it, and how to get it back or have it deleted. Written in sentences rather than clauses.

On this page

    1. Who we are

    Amritha Traditional Spice Mill Pvt Ltd is a spice mill at 48 Heritage Spice Quarter, Bazaar Road, Mattancherry, Kochi 682002, Kerala. We are the data controller for the information described here. Where we process on behalf of a business customer — for example a private-label account — we act as a processor under that customer's instructions, and their privacy policy governs the relationship with their end customers.

    Our designated contact for anything in this document is the trade desk: mill@amrithaspices.in, or by post at the address above marked for the attention of the data protection contact.

    2. What we collect, and only when you give it to us

    We do not run advertising trackers, we do not sell data, and we do not buy contact lists. Everything we hold arrives because you sent it through a form, an email, a phone call or an order.

    Enquiries and quotations

    • Your name, email address and, if you gave it, a phone number.
    • What you asked for: product, volume, packaging, deadline.
    • Any documents or photographs you attached.

    Orders and the batch record

    • Delivery address, billing address, GST or trade registration details.
    • Purchase history, so we can reproduce a previous batch on request.
    • Batch and lot numbers, which is how a batch card is traced back to the field.

    Newsletter

    • Your email address and the date you gave it to us.

    Nothing else. No reading tracking, no open tracking, no behavioural profiling, and no pixels anywhere on this site.

    Mill visits

    • Your name, contact details and the date of the visit, because we keep a register at the gate.

    3. Why we are allowed to use it

    We rely on three lawful bases, and we would rather say which one applies than hide behind a general statement.

    • Performance of a contract. Order details, delivery addresses, and everything needed to mill, pack, ship and stand behind a batch.
    • Legitimate interests. Answering an enquiry that has not yet become an order, keeping the batch record safe, and preventing fraud on trade accounts. We have assessed that these do not override your rights, and you can object at any time.
    • Consent. The newsletter, and nothing else. Consent is specific, withdrawable in one click, and never a condition of an order.

    4. Who else sees it

    Only three categories of organisation, and only where they are genuinely needed:

    • Payment providers. Card and bank details go straight to them; we never see or store a full card number.
    • Couriers and freight forwarders. The name, address and phone number needed to deliver. Nothing else.
    • Our hosting and email providers. They process the site and the newsletter on our instructions, under contract, in India.

    We do not sell, rent or share your details with advertisers, list brokers or aggregators. If a law court compels disclosure, we will tell you unless we are forbidden from doing so.

    5. How long we keep it

    • Enquiries that do not become orders: 24 months, then deleted.
    • Order and batch records: seven years from the last delivery, because traceability on a food product has a legal life.
    • Newsletter subscription: until you unsubscribe, plus 30 days of suppression data so we do not accidentally re-add you.
    • Gate register for mill visits: 12 months.

    6. Cookies and local storage

    This site sets no advertising or analytics cookies. Appearance and text-direction preferences — light or dark, left-to-right or right-to-left — are stored in your browser's localStorage so the site remembers them. They never leave your device, they contain no identifier, and clearing your browser data removes them.

    If you ever want an operational cookie — for example, to keep a form half-finished — we will say so here and give you a way to refuse it.

    7. Orders outside India

    We ship to 34 countries. When an order leaves India, the delivery address, contact number and any customs paperwork cross a border as part of the consignment, handled by the freight forwarder we have appointed. Everything stays on infrastructure located in India otherwise; we do not use cloud services hosted outside the country for customer data.

    8. How it is kept safe

    • Access to customer records is limited to the people who need it for the work in front of them, and reviewed when someone leaves.
    • Paper records — invoices, gate register, trade applications — are kept in locked storage at the mill, not in a corridor.
    • Passwords, where they exist, are hashed. We do not store anything we could read aloud.
    • We would rather admit a breach than hide one. If a breach affects your data, we will tell you and the relevant authority without waiting for permission.

    9. Your rights

    You can ask us for any of the following, and we will not charge you or make you jump through hoops:

    • A copy of everything we hold about you.
    • Correction of anything inaccurate — including your address on a standing order.
    • Deletion, where we are not required to keep the record for a legal reason.
    • Restriction of processing while a question about accuracy is resolved.
    • Objection to any use we are relying on legitimate interests for.
    • Withdrawal of consent for the newsletter, at any time, with no consequence to any order.

    Write to mill@amrithaspices.in with the subject line Data request. We answer within 30 days, usually within three working days.

    10. Children

    This site and our services are for businesses and for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has sent us information, write to us and we will delete it.

    11. If this policy changes

    We will change this policy when what we do changes, not before. The date at the top of the page is the last revision. If a change materially affects how we use information you have already given us, we will email the people it affects before it takes effect rather than burying it in a revision note.

    12. Complaints

    If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, tell us first — the trade desk has the authority to fix most things on the spot. If you want to take it further, the relevant authority in India is the Data Protection Board.

    Everything in this document is also available in Malayalam and Tamil on request, and we will read it to you over the phone if that is easier.

    ANYTHING UNCLEAR

    Ask Before You Assume

    Privacy language exists to be read by lawyers and skipped by everyone else. If any of this is unclear, ask the trade desk and we will explain it in plain words, in the language you prefer, without a call being recorded or scored.
