Ask Us to Measure Something

Half the articles here started as a question from a customer, usually a slightly suspicious one. If you want to know whether a claim about spice is true, or whether two lots are genuinely different, ask. We will run the test and publish the numbers, including the ones that make us look ordinary.

  • “Is this really single-origin?” We will test it and tell you what the fingerprint says.
  • “Why does my masala taste different every batch?” Usually granule size. Occasionally something worse.
  • “Can you blend this at 40% less heat?” Yes, and we will explain exactly what changes when you do.
