THE MILL, IN FOUR CLAIMS

Why the Same Spice Smells Different Here

These are the four commitments behind every line in the catalogue, and the four we are willing to be measured against.

  • Temperature is logged, not assumed. A probe thermometer on every batch, recorded on the batch card.
  • Raw material is traceable to a field. District, grower and harvest window travel with the spice.
  • Nothing is blended to hit a price. If a lot is not good enough we decline the lot, not the standard.
  • Grind profiles are calibrated stones. Four verified granule sizes from one set of runners.

FROM FIELD TO FOLDED PACK

The Common Process

✦
01

Source

Direct contracts with 2,400 farming families across Salem, Guntur, Ramganj Mandi, Unjha and the Idukki highlands. No brokers in the chain.

02

Inspect

Moisture, foreign matter, volatile oil and curcumin measured on arrival. A failing lot goes back to the grower, with a phone call, not a rejection slip.

03

Mill cold

Slow granite runners at low RPM. Batch temperature recorded before and after. If the run exceeds 38 °C the batch is reground, not shipped.

04

Prove & pack

Particle-size check, organoleptic sniff by two millers, then nitrogen-flushed packing with the batch card folded into the lid.
