WHAT EVERY PLAN INCLUDES

The Three Promises

✦

Reground free, 14 days

If a batch is not right, tell us within fourteen days and we re-mill it at no charge. It is almost always granule size, and getting that right is our job, not yours.

Under 38 °C, always

Every batch is probed and logged. If a run goes over 38 °C it is reground before dispatch, and the batch card says so either way.

No adulteration, ever

No fillers, no carriers, no volatile extracts, and no cheaper lot blended into a premium one. If the standard moves, we decline the lot.

TRANSPARENCY, BRIEFLY

What Actually Moves Your Price

A spice price quoted over the phone is close to meaningless without these three. Ask any supplier what changes their quote and watch whether they answer with all three.

What changes the number, and by how much it typically moves.
Factor Typical movement What we do about it
Raw material ± 15–60% Single-origin lots are priced at harvest, not averaged. We tell you the district and the window.
Granule profile ± 5–12% Four verified profiles. Finer profiles cost more to produce and sieve out more.
Packaging ± 4–38% Plain foil pouch to printed rigid box. Private-label tooling is quoted separately.
Volume Up to −22% Break points at 25 kg, 100 kg and 500 kg per month.
Lead time ± 0–15% Under 48 hours attracts a rush charge on custom blends. We will always offer you the slower option.

We do not publish per-kilogram prices as if they were facts. They move with every harvest, and a number that looks authoritative but is eight months old helps nobody.
