THE ART OF FRESHLY GROUND SPICES

From Whole Spice to
Pure Aroma

Commercial processing strips spices of their natural soul. We preserve the living volatile oils through centuries-old stone grinding techniques.

At Amritha, every whole spice is sun-dried under gentle warmth, inspected by hand to eliminate impurities, and milled in slow-turning granite chakkis. Because our millstones never overheat, the aromatic thymol, curcumin, piperine, and essential cineole stay locked in the powder—delivering an unmistakable burst of fragrance the moment your pan warms.

  • ✓

    Small Batch Handcrafting: Ground in 5kg micro-batches to guarantee peak aroma and freshness.

  • ✓

    Single-Origin Provenance: Sourced directly from heirloom farmers in Malabar, Salem, and Guntur.

  • ✓

    Zero Adulterants: No synthetic starch, added anti-caking agents, artificial coloring, or preservatives.
